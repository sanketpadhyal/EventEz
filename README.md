## 💡 What is EventEz?

EventEz is a college-focused event management platform created to simplify the organization of fests, seminars, workshops, and campus activities.  
It aims to replace manual coordination, spreadsheets, and WhatsApp chaos with a structured and professional system.

---

## ✨ Planned Features

- 🎟️ Online event registration & ticketing  
- 👥 Volunteer and team role management  
- 📢 Real-time announcements and updates  
- 🗓️ Event schedules and timelines  
- 🔐 Secure authentication for students & organizers  
- 📱 Mobile-first, responsive UI  

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack (Planned / In Use)

- **Frontend:** React.js, CSS  
- **Backend:** Firebase / API-based services (planned)  
- **Database:** Firestore (planned)  
- **Hosting:** Netlify  

---

## 🧠 Why EventEz?

Managing college events shouldn’t be chaotic.  
EventEz is built to bring **clarity, structure, and professionalism** to campus events while keeping the experience simple for students and organizers.
